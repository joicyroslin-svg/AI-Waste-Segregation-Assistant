⚠️ Responsible AI & Privacy Notice
Transparency & Uncertainty

Every response includes a confidence level (High / Medium / Low). When the assistant cannot confidently identify an item, it will ask for more information rather than guess. Confidence is determined by how closely your input matches the knowledge base — it is not a guarantee.

Image Input Limitation

This prototype cannot analyse the visual content of an image. When you upload a photo, the assistant uses only the filename as a text hint. Classification accuracy is therefore limited — results will be capped at Medium confidence and clearly labelled as image-based estimates.

Privacy & Data Handling

No text input, image, or personal information is sent to any server. All processing happens locally in your browser. Images are loaded for preview only and are not stored, transmitted, or retained after you close or refresh the page.

No Unsupported Claims

Sustainability tips in this prototype are based on general, widely-accepted waste management principles. Specific statistics are not cited unless they are well-established and sourced. When in doubt, the assistant will use qualitative language rather than unverified figures.
